# Place Explorer update

- Craft Map now has a place search directly on the page.
- Enter any state, city, country or region (for example Haryana or Rajasthan).
- The AI Craft Guide returns crafts, activities, food, places, festivals, best time and a fallback explanation when the place is not known for a major craft.
- Live Supabase craft-region and artisan records remain the source for map markers.
- The page no longer becomes blank just because `craft_regions` is empty.
- Quick Indian state buttons are informational discovery shortcuts, not marketplace/demo records.
